Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your data from the HappyRabbits AI coaching platform, you can do so by following these steps:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account's Settings & Privacy. Click Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Apps and Websites.
  3. Find HappyRabbits and click the Remove button.
  4. To delete your AI coaching history from our servers: Please send an email to happyrabbits.coach.1@gmail.com with the subject line "Request Data Deletion."

Upon receiving your email, our team will purge your user profile, planning data, and AI interaction logs from our databases within 48 hours.
