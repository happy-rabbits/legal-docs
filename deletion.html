Data Deletion Instructions

At HappyRabbits, we respect your privacy and your right to control your data. Below are the instructions to remove our app's access to your accounts and request a permanent deletion of your AI coaching history.

1. Facebook Connection

  1. Log into your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left-side menu, click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Find HappyRabbits and click the Remove button.

2. Google Connection

  1. Go to your Google Account Security Settings.
  2. Under "Third-party apps with account access," select HappyRabbits.
  3. Click Remove Access.

3. Permanent Data Purge (HappyRabbits AI)

Removing access via Facebook or Google stops the app from syncing, but it may not automatically delete existing coaching logs on our servers. To permanently wipe your AI planning data and chat history:

We will process your request and purge all database records related to your account within 48 hours.
